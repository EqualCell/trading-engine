05 / EVENT HISTORY

What happened

    Built to explain, not predict.

    One-unit fixed-spread quotes. Explicit refreshes only. Cancel-and-replace loses FIFO priority. Quotes may execute immediately.

    No buying-power checks, fees, latency, margin, funding costs, or self-trade prevention. Negative balances are allowed; resting orders reserve no funds.

    Observed market activity, not a strategy backtest.

    Snapshots replace the displayed depth. Recorded trades do not decrement snapshots. No queue or complete order-level book is reconstructed.

    Latest 100 recorded trades and 50 event details are shown. Charts sample only selected-event history into up to 240 buckets, preserving bucket endpoints and every missing-state boundary; all events remain step-accessible.
